WEBCAM DRAW

How to use it

Hold your hand up to the camera, a foot or so back, with good lighting. Each pose below controls the board.
  • 1
    Draw Raise just your index finger and move it to paint a line.
  • 2
    Blue Raise index + middle finger to switch to blue.
  • 3
    Red Raise index, middle + ring finger to switch to red.
  • 4
    Green Raise four fingers, not the thumb, to switch to green.
  • 5
    Erase Open your whole hand, all five fingers up, and pass it over a stroke to remove it.
  • 0
    Pause Close your hand into a fist to stop drawing without erasing.
Use the slider to change brush thickness, Clear to wipe the board, and Save PNG to download your drawing. Tracking runs locally in your browser, no video leaves your machine.
