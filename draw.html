WEBCAM DRAW

How to use it

Hold your hand up to the camera, a foot or so back, with good lighting. Each pose below controls the board.
  • 1
    Draw Raise just your index finger and move it to paint a line.
  • 2
    Blue Raise index + middle finger to switch to blue.
  • 3
    Red Raise index, middle + ring finger to switch to red.
